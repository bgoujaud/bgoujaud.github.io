I am an Assistant Professor in the [Citi](https://www.telecom-sudparis.eu/en/citi/department-team/) team at Telecom SudParis, supported by a [Hi! PARIS](https://hi-paris.fr/) Chair.

Previously, I completed a [PhD](https://theses.hal.science/tel-04746799) in the [CMAP](https://cmap.ip-paris.fr/) lab at Ecole Polytechnique under the supervision of [Aymeric Dieuleveut](http://www.cmap.polytechnique.fr/~aymeric.dieuleveut/) (Ecole Polytechnique) and [Adrien Taylor](https://adrientaylor.github.io) (INRIA), and was a postdoctoral researcher in the [MIND](https://www.inria.fr/en/mind) team at INRIA Saclay, working on Optimization with [Thomas Moreau](https://tommoral.github.io/about.html) (INRIA) and [Pierre Ablin](https://pierreablin.com/) (Apple).

My research interests include Machine Learning, Optimization, and Statistics. In particular, I am interested in distributed learning, generalization, privacy, robustness, diffusion processes, and related topics.
